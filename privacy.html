Woof Box - Dog Sudoku

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 20 November 2026

EDM Games ("we", "us", or "our") develops Woof Box - Dog Sudoku (the "game"), Android package com.edenming.woofbox. This policy explains how information is handled by the game, its advertising and consent providers, this website, and our support email. Contact us at edenmings@gmail.com.

In brief: your game progress is stored locally, and the game has no accounts or developer-operated cloud saves. The game is not data-free: Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process information for ads, consent, measurement, and fraud prevention. We have not implemented our own level-completion telemetry service or BigQuery reporting.

1. Local game information

The game stores progress and preferences in local files on your device. This can include completed levels, daily challenge progress, Paw Power, hints, unlocked items, equipped items, reward-track progress, language, and sound settings. We do not upload these save files to our own servers or provide accounts, sign-in, or a developer-operated cloud-save service.

Android or a device provider may back up or restore app data according to your device settings and the app's backup configuration. Such system-level services are separate from a cloud-save service operated by us.

The current game has no in-app purchases and does not request your payment details. Paw Power is earned through gameplay.

2. Advertising and consent information

The game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads, and Google UMP to manage applicable privacy messages and consent choices.

Depending on your location, age-related treatment, device settings, consent, and the advertising service configuration, these services may collect or share:

  • IP address and approximate location: an IP address can be used to estimate general location. The game does not request GPS or precise-location permission.
  • Device or other identifiers: such as the Android advertising ID (when available and permitted), app set ID, and other identifiers processed by the advertising service.
  • App and ad interactions: such as app launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views. This is separate from a developer-operated upload of your puzzle progress.
  • Diagnostic information: information about app or SDK performance, such as launch time, hangs, errors or crash-related diagnostics where collected, and energy usage.
  • Privacy choices: consent status and signals used to remember and apply the relevant advertising and privacy choices.

These services use information to deliver and measure ads, provide permitted personalization, understand service performance, apply privacy choices, and prevent fraud or abuse. Non-personalized or limited ads do not necessarily mean that no data is processed.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data-disclosure information for further details.

3. Support and website information

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to send. We use these to respond, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests. Please send only information needed to explain your issue.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or contact forms to this site. GitHub may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure hosting under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Our email provider also processes messages to deliver and store correspondence.

4. Use, sharing, and retention

We use local save information to make the game function and retain your progress. Advertising and consent information is processed by Google and, where applicable, its advertising partners for the purposes described above. Support information is handled by us and the service providers needed to receive and reply to your message. We may also disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, or prevent abuse.

Local save data remains until it is cleared, removed, or overwritten. System backups may have separate retention rules. We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the matter, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations; you may contact us to request deletion. Google and other service providers apply their own retention rules to information they process; we do not control their retention periods or promise deletion of their records.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Local progress: Android Settings → Apps → Woof Box - Dog Sudoku → Storage → Clear storage can remove local game data and reset progress. Uninstalling generally removes the app's local data, but device backups may restore data later. Review Android backup settings if needed.
  • Advertising ID: Android's privacy / ads settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID where supported. Menu names vary by device and Android version. This does not necessarily stop all advertising or data processing.
  • Consent: where applicable, the game uses UMP privacy messages to present choices. Use the privacy-options entry if made available in your version. If you cannot find how to revisit a choice, contact us for help.
  • Rewarded ads: you may decline an optional rewarded ad; you will not receive the associated extra hint.
  • Requests: depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or request portability. Contact us to exercise applicable rights relating to information we handle, such as support emails. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

The game has no user accounts, so there is no game-account deletion process. This does not remove your rights concerning other information. We cannot identify or erase all advertising-provider records from a local game save; for data held independently by Google, refer to Google's privacy controls and request processes. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

6. Young people

The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults, and is not directed to children under 13. Local rules may impose additional protections or consent requirements for teenagers; an intended audience of 13+ is not a statement that all teenagers are adults or able to consent everywhere.

If you believe information about a child has been provided to us inappropriately, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action concerning information under our control.

7. Security and international processing

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission system is completely secure. Keep your device and operating system updated.

Google, GitHub, and our email provider may process information in countries other than where you live, according to their policies and applicable safeguards.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change, and will update the date above. If we introduce features such as developer-operated level-completion reporting, we will update the relevant disclosures and provide notices or obtain consent where required before the new processing begins.
