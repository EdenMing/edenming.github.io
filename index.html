Woof Box -
Dog Sudoku

A dog-themed logic puzzle game. Read the grid, make your marks, and work your way to a solution.

A little progress, one puzzle at a time.

Solve levels to earn Paw Power, then spend it on dogs, skins, music, and scenes in the Paw Shop. Equip your favourites in the Kennel and return to the next puzzle.

Woof Box includes main levels, daily challenges, a First Steps reward track, and English and Chinese interfaces. It is designed for teen and adult puzzle players, not for children under 13.

Local progress. No account needed.
Game progress is saved on your device. The current game has no in-app purchases; Paw Power is earned through play.

The game includes ads and optional rewarded ads for hints. Google advertising and consent services may process data as explained in our privacy policy.

Release status

We are preparing the Android version for Google Play. A store link will be added when it is available.
